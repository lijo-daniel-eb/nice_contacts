## Long Description
Take control of your contacts with a smarter, faster contacts experience.

My Contacts combines beautiful contact management with intelligent on-device analysis to help you clean, organize, and act faster.

What makes it different:
- Smart Insights Hub: Get actionable suggestions, duplicate detection, smart grouping, contact insights, and cleanup recommendations in one place.
- Duplicate Detection That Works: Detect potential duplicates using multiple signals (name similarity, phone match, and email match), not just exact names.
- Built for Big Contact Lists: Fast loading and smooth scrolling even with large address books thanks to optimized caching and background processing.
- Smarter Search and Actions: Search by name or number, then call or message quickly with swipe actions.
- Contact-Aware Recording Discovery: Match call recordings to contacts using both name and number heuristics.
- Fake Call Scheduler: Schedule simulated incoming calls for situations where you need a practical escape flow.
- Clear Save Location Guidance: See where new contacts are likely to be saved (device default account behavior).

Privacy-first approach:
- Contact intelligence runs on-device.
- No unnecessary cloud dependency for core analysis flows.
